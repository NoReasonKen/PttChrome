# Improve Read and Unread Article Contrast on Android

- Date: 2026-10-25 (Asia/Taipei)
- Status: Idea, not implemented

## Problem

In the installed Android app, white text can feel somewhat low in distinguishability, though it is normally acceptable. In the article list, enabling `dimReadArticles` (「已讀文章低亮顯示」) makes the text colors for read and unread article titles too similar to tell apart at a glance.

## Desired behavior

Make read and unread article titles more clearly distinguishable at a glance in the article list, while keeping both readable. The primary context is the easy-reading article list on Android.

## Open choices

- Which colors or brightness levels should distinguish the two states?
- Should unread titles become brighter, read titles become dimmer, or should both change?
- Should the adjustment apply outside the installed app as well?
