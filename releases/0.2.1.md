PttChrome Fork Android 0.2.1

## Highlights

- Add APK updates from About, with version checks, background downloads and handoff to the system installer.
- Organize About with version sources, bilingual release summaries and clear update status indicators.
- Make unread articles easier to identify and add JPTT-style left bars to article lists.
- Show the board, posting time and author in the article app bar, removing the duplicate information below it.
- Fix Back failing to close settings from the category list.
