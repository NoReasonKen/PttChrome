PttChrome Fork Android 0.4.0

## Highlights

- Add opt-in Pixiv numeric-ID lookup: after enabling it in Enhancements, tap a standalone number in an article or comment to preview the matching Pixiv user and artwork and open their pages. Requires this APK.
- Back now shows the board list, article list, search results or source article below exactly as the reader left it, keeping order, selection, scroll position and read marks instead of merging PTT's returned page. Taps, long presses, the toolbar, the drawer and further swipe-backs respond before PTT catches up; actions that need PTT run once it does.
- Returning to a list briefly shows a new-articles notice when PTT has newer posts, and articles posted meanwhile load as the reader scrolls down.
- Back from same-subject or same-author search keeps the source article's reading position and image previews and appends comments added meanwhile; an edited article shows an updated notice, and a source that cannot be reopened stays shown without sending comments. Search results now slide in from the right, and no reconnect notices appear.
- Swipe gestures choose their direction only after a short travel, so scrolling is less often mistaken for a swipe, and swipe-back starts faster.
- Restore defaults asks for confirmation and moves to the end of About on narrow screens. Fix dialog Back reaching PTT, toolbar taps ignored during menu animations or after Back, a newly opened article not starting at the top, stale Settings category scroll, and Back occasionally leaving the site. Debug recordings also log page errors.
