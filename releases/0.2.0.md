PttChrome Fork Android 0.2.0

## Highlights

- Add an article shortcut drawer for thread/author searches and previous, first, and next thread articles.
- Support swipe-back on articles, article lists, and supported board lists, with retained pages and reading positions during transitions.
- Return through nested author/thread searches one result or source article at a time.
- Preserve article/list easy reading, search conditions, and reading position across rotation.
- Restore search results and reading position after reopening or reconnecting, and save locations before shutdown.
- Fix images appearing unexpectedly enlarged when they finish loading after a fast viewer swipe.
