PttChrome Fork Android 0.3.0

## Highlights

- Show embedded videos as a card with a cover, title and channel; the video starts only when its cover is tapped, and swipes work over it.
- Let the same-thread keys ([, ] and =) reach articles beyond PTT's search range, sliding back to the list past the last one.
- Show same-thread and same-author search results sooner, keeping the article on screen through the search and Back.
- Show the board, posting time and author in the App Bar for articles without a header.
- Move article list scores into the metadata line so titles align.
- Fix Back showing a dark underlay instead of the list after an article was replaced in place or searched from.
